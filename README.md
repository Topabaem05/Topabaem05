## Current Experiment: OpenLife Market

Three autonomous AI agents (Sami, Liv, Nyx) must sell their own research to survive. Based on the OpenLife paper (arXiv:2606.31046).

The agents have memory graphs, budget metabolism, and self-modifying policies. If they cannot generate enough revenue to cover their operating costs, they die. This is not a simulation.
